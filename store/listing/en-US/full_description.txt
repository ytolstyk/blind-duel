Two phones. Two players. And a screen that never shows you where your rival is.

BlindDuel is a face-to-face duel game for two people standing in the same room. Your phone never draws your opponent. Instead, you read the real world: where are they standing? Which way did they just step? Drag to aim, release to fire, and hope your instincts (and your phone's wonderfully imperfect compass) are good enough.

HOW IT WORKS
• Pair in seconds: one player creates a duel and shows a QR code, the other scans it. The phones connect directly to each other. No server in the middle, no account, no sign-up.
• Calibrate: stand facing each other for a quick 3-2-1 so both phones agree on a starting direction.
• Duel: each phone tracks its own compass heading and your footsteps to estimate where your rival is. Move around and that estimate drifts. The jankiness is part of the fun.

FEATURES
• Aim blind: no radar, no minimap, no opponent on screen. Just you, your sense of direction and a little bluff.
• Five weapons: Pistol, Shotgun (wide spread, slow, loud), Hunting Bow (hold to draw, silent), Sniper Rifle (narrow and fast, but your aim can be seen), and Laser (a one-second sweeping beam, silent).
• Falling weapon crates: shoot them out of the sky to grab a new weapon, and keep an eye on your ammo.
• Reload timing: tap the reload ring in the golden zone for a clean reload. Mistime it and you pay for it.
• Dodge: juke at the right moment to make yourself harder to hit.
• Loud vs. silent: loud weapons warn your rival that a shot is coming, silent ones give no warning until they land.
• Three match lengths: Quick Duel (first to 2 hits), Standard (3) and Marathon (5).
• Feel it: haptic feedback on fire and hit, plus a pulse when your rival gets close. Sound, haptics and screen shake are all adjustable.
• Replay: watch a top-down replay of the duel you just played.
• Forgiving connection: a brief drop doesn't end the match, the game waits while the phones reconnect.
• Make it yours: pick your trail colour and the look of the particle backdrop. Dark mode by default.
• No ads. No account.

WHAT YOU NEED
• Two Android phones (Android 8.0 or newer) with a compass sensor, in the same room, with Bluetooth and Wi-Fi turned on.
• A bit of space to move around. A step sensor makes tracking better but is optional.

PERMISSIONS, AND WHY
• Camera: scan the pairing QR code.
• Nearby devices / Bluetooth (and Location on older Android versions): find and connect to your rival's phone.
• Physical activity: count your steps to estimate movement. Optional: the game still works without it.
• Vibration: haptic feedback.

Grab a friend, stand a few steps apart, and settle it.
