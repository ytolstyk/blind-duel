# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

## [1.2] - 2026-11-24
### Added
- `scripts/release.sh`: one-command release packaging (runs tests, builds AAB + APK, copies them with R8 `mapping.txt`, checksums and release notes into `releases/v<version>/`, prints a link). `app/build.gradle.kts` now signs release builds when a gitignored `keystore.properties` is present.
- Play Store listing assets under `store/`: 512px icon, 1024x500 feature graphic, 8 captioned screenshots each for phone / 7" tablet / 10" tablet, and the en-US title + short/full description. Includes the scripts to regenerate them (`store/tools/`: emulator capture, composer, icon/feature generator, and a validator that checks Play's size/format/length rules).
- Match length: a new host-only "Match length" picker on the Create screen (Quick Duel / Standard / Marathon, 2/3/5 hits to win) applied when creating a duel. The joiner automatically adopts the host's choice — there is no separate joiner-side picker, and Solo Test Mode always plays at the standard 3 hits regardless of the picker's current selection.
- Dodge: a new HUD button (bottom-left of the Duel screen) that briefly narrows the opponent's effective hit tolerance against you when tapped, on a cooldown. Rewards ducking/juking out of the way of an incoming shot rather than standing still.
- Fog-of-war confidence decay: the longer you go without taking a physical step, the more forgiving your own aim's hit tolerance becomes — an intentional mechanic formalizing the existing "your sensor estimate gets worse over time" jankiness. Not shown in the UI.
- "Leave Practice" button on the Duel screen while in Solo Test Mode, so a practice session can be exited without playing it out.
- Player name entered on the main menu is now persisted (DataStore) and pre-filled on future launches.
- Reworked the Duel screen's ambient particle backdrop into twinkling parallax stars plus falling dust that swirls when the phone physically moves (new accelerometer-driven `MotionProvider.tiltUpdates()`).
- Projectiles now draw a wobbling flame-style tail, push nearby dust particles away as they travel, and trigger a camera shake on firing and on taking a hit.
- Added an incoming-projectile visual for shots fired at you (flies in from your own live bearing estimate to the opponent; misses now visibly fly past and off-screen instead of only showing a flash).
- Solo Test Mode now periodically simulates an unprompted opponent shot (occasionally a hit) so the incoming-projectile and hit-flash visuals can be previewed without needing to fire first.
- An incoming hit now triggers an expanding, fading explosion burst on the character instead of the projectile just vanishing on arrival.
- Landing a hit on the opponent now shows a fading "HIT" indicator, since the opponent's character is never rendered on screen and this was previously the only feedback missing that a shot connected.
- The opponent's health is now shown alongside your own (top-end, labeled "ENEMY") instead of only your own health being visible.
- Added a magazine/reload mechanic: the basic weapon now holds 3 rounds before requiring a reload. Reloading is shown as a ring with a dial that sweeps around once over its duration; tapping the ring while the dial is inside a highlighted "perfect zone" (50%-80% of the way through) completes the reload instantly, while a mistimed tap extends it instead. Reload can also be started voluntarily and early by tapping the new ammo-pip HUD row, forfeiting any rounds left in the magazine. A brief "RELOADED" pulse plays on completion. The prior per-shot fire cooldown has been removed entirely — the magazine/reload cycle is now the only pacing lever between shots, so all 3 rounds can be fired back-to-back before a reload is required.
- Added four new weapons on top of the base pistol, picked up from wobbling weapon crates that fall independently on each player's own screen: **shotgun** (3 shells, wide spread, slow, loud), **hunting bow** (4 arrows, requires a brief hold to draw before firing, speed scales with how far you pull back, silent), **sniper rifle** (2 shots, narrow/precise tolerance, fast, and broadcasts a live aim indicator the opponent can see and react to), and **laser** (1 charge, a 1-second drag-to-sweep continuous beam that scores at most one hit per activation, silent). A crate weapon is never refilled — once its single magazine is spent (naturally or via an early manual reload), the player automatically reverts to the pistol.
- Loud weapons (pistol, shotgun, sniper) always telegraph an incoming shot to the opponent even on a miss, as before; the two new silent weapons (bow, laser) give the opponent zero warning unless they're actually hit.
- Added placeholder per-weapon fire sound cues via Android's built-in tone generator (no audio assets exist in the project yet — swap in real sound effects later).
- The aim line and character now render at reduced opacity while out of ammo (mid-reload or a spent single-charge weapon), since aiming still works during a reload for crate-targeting purposes even though firing doesn't.
- Weapon crates now fall more slowly, giving more time to react and aim.
- Destroying a weapon crate now requires actually shooting it: firing a shot whose aim ray passes near the crate spends a round and, once the projectile reaches it, blows it up (screen shake, particle burst, color flash) before granting the new weapon — replacing the old "release your drag near the crate" free-grab.
- Each weapon's aim line now previews its actual firing behavior instead of a plain line for every weapon: shotgun shows a widening cone, sniper shows a target reticle at the tip, bow shows an arrowhead, and laser (which fires immediately on touch) shows only its full sweeping beam.
- Taking a hit now sprays a directional burst of debris particles off the side of the character facing the incoming shot, and the incoming projectile now visibly shrinks/fades into the character as it arrives instead of just popping on contact.
- New Settings screen sections: Sound volume, Haptic intensity, and Screen shake intensity sliders (0-100%, shake up to 200%), plus Trail theme (Violet/Ember/Mint, recolors your own outgoing projectile trail) and Particle theme (Classic/Dusk/Neon, recolors the ambient star/dust backdrop) pickers. All persist across launches and apply live if changed mid-duel.
- Added a distinct short haptic pulse on firing, and a "getting close" haptic pulse when the estimated distance to the opponent crosses into a nearer zone (based on the existing dead-reckoning step model) — both scaled by the new haptic intensity setting.
- Weapon fire sound cues now have a real volume control, and landing/missing a shot now plays a distinct hit or miss tone (previously only firing had a sound).
- Reconnect-on-drop grace period: a transient Nearby Connections signal loss during Calibration or Duel now pauses the session (a "Reconnecting…" overlay, input gated) and silently retries reconnecting for up to 8 seconds before ending the session, instead of ending it immediately on any signal blip. Calibration's one-shot handshake messages have no redelivery, so a bounded timeout on each awaiting phase falls back to a clean session end (rather than hanging forever) if the message that completes the handshake was lost during the gap.
- Connection quality indicator: a small Good/Fair/Poor dot on the Duel screen, derived from jitter in the opponent's motion-update arrival timing, giving an early warning before a full drop.
- Spectator/replay mode: after a duel ends, "Watch Replay" on the result screen plays back a zoomed-out top-down view of both players as dots moving through real 2D space (from each phone's own recorded motion data), each with a small rotated rectangle showing facing direction, plus hit flashes and a live health readout synced to playback position. In-memory only for the match just played; not persisted across app restarts.
- More fanfare on the result screen (and Solo Test Mode, which shares it): losing now bursts the central avatar into radiating debris while "YOU"/"LOSE" fly apart to rest near the top/bottom of the screen; winning now shows balloons endlessly rising in the background, each poppable by tapping it — just for fun, with no effect on rematch/quit.

### Changed
- Moved the match-length picker and the debug-only Solo Test entry point (starting weapon, crate toggle) off the main menu and onto the Create screen, so the main menu stays to just name entry and Create/Join.

### Fixed
- A popped victory balloon's particle burst was centered and sized off the balloon's full square width, but the balloon body itself is drawn shorter (an oval at 92% of that height) — the burst now centers on the actual balloon body and uses named `GameConstants` tunables instead of inline magic numbers.
- Solo Test Mode (practice) never tracked the practice dummy's health, so landing hits never ended the duel or showed the win screen. `LoopbackGameConnection` now applies damage to a simulated opponent and echoes back `HealthUpdate`s, so 3 hits now resolves a win exactly like a real duel.
- Top-anchored UI (settings icon, headers, health dots, hint chips, etc.) across every screen no longer sits underneath the system status bar; each screen now insets its content with `statusBarsPadding()` while backgrounds stay edge-to-edge.
- Tapping "Rematch" after a Solo Test Mode duel hung on "Waiting for opponent…" forever, since rematch re-enters Calibration without re-hosting and `LoopbackGameConnection` only auto-sent the opponent's `ReadyForDuel` on the very first connect. It now echoes `ReadyForDuel` back like it already does for `RematchRequest`, so a rematch starts a new practice duel correctly.
- Solo Test Mode's simulated opponent shots always defaulted to the pistol's cosmetics/timing (`GameMessage.FireEvent`'s weaponType/travelDurationMs were never set), so incoming shots in practice never showed the shotgun's pellet spread, the sniper/laser telltale, or the bow's silent/slower travel a real opponent using those weapons would produce. `LoopbackGameConnection` now picks a random weapon per simulated shot for parity with a real duel.
